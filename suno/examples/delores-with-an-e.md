# Delores with an E
Titles: Delores with an E / Nobody Here by That Name / Ray Pays the Bill

## STYLE
```
Art Rock, Post-Punk, late 70s, spoken word, uneasy, deadpan, 82 BPM, one female voice throughout, dry spoken-word delivery close to the mic, sung passages in a low plain alto with held open vowels, muted fingerpicked bass, brushed snare, detuned upright piano, a mechanical rotary telephone bell, small room ambience, warm tape saturation, sparse arrangement with long gaps between phrases. Through-composed, each section its own shape, three short sung passages inside a spoken narrative. The rotary bell rings twice in the intro, bass alone under the first verse, piano enters at the interlude, brushed drums enter at the bridge, everything drops out for the a cappella last two lines. Ends on a hard stop.
```

## LYRICS
```
[Intro - rotary bell, bass, instrumental only]

[Verse 1 - spoken, close mic]
Three in the morning, Pruitt Street,
and something in the hall is ringing.
Not yours. Yours is face down on the rug by the couch.
This is a bell. A hammer on metal. Two notes.
Nothing rings like that anymore,
and who the hell still has this number.

[Verse 2 - spoken, bass enters]
It's the yellow one. Mustard yellow.
The dial takes all day to come back around.
Hall table. The doily she crocheted.
It's been there longer than you've been alive.
Two years since the funeral and the line was never cut.
Ray pays it. First of every month.
Nobody asks Ray why.

[Interlude - sung, low, held notes]
Let it ring, that's the rule.
Wrong number, let it ring.
The bell doesn't know she's gone.
You were raised to pick up.

[Verse 3 - spoken, drier, tighter]
You pick up. The receiver weighs what a receiver used to weigh.
The cord's still twisted the way she twisted it,
round one finger, every call she ever took.
You say hello. Your voice is wrong in this hallway.
A man. Older than you. Older than Ray.
He says, is Delores there.
With an E. You hear the E.

[Bridge - sung, full voice, drums enter]
Her name was Dolores, with an O.
Nobody's said the E out loud
since nineteen seventy-one.
Before Ray. Before the doily.
Before she was anyone you'd know.
Somebody kept the E in his mouth
and dialed her at three in the morning.

[Verse 4 - spoken, music thins]
You'd take a message, if you'd ever met her.
You say, nobody here by that name.
Which is true. Which has been true since seventy-one.
He says sorry. He doesn't leave his.
The receiver goes back on the cradle.
You straighten the doily.
You don't tell Ray.
You don't unplug it either.

[Outro - sung, a cappella, one voice]
Delores with an E.
Nobody here by that name.
[End]
```
