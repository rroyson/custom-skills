# Clutch Is a Conversation
Titles: Clutch Is a Conversation / Forty Stalls / Route Nine

## STYLE
```
Hip-Hop, Boom-Bap, 90s East Coast, reflective, warm, head-nod, 90 BPM, male rapper, unhurried conversational storytelling flow, mid-range chest voice, clear diction, rapped hook, half-spoken bridge, dusty chopped soul sample, cracking snare, deep round kick, muted upright bass line, light record scratches on the hook, vinyl crackle, lo-fi grit, punchy dry drums, close dry vocal. Drums drop out under the bridge for a close half-spoken vocal, then the full beat returns with doubled vocals on the final chorus. Ends on a hard stop after two spoken lines.
```

## EXCLUDE STYLES
```
trap hi-hats, 808 slides, autotune, EDM synths, orchestral strings, choir, sung R&B hook
```

## LYRICS
```
[Intro - drum break, 4 bars, instrumental]

[Verse 1 - laid back, storytelling]
Summer 'fore he shipped, Marcus tossed me the keys to the Civic
'98, five-speed, Black Ice tree off the rear-view, I'm in it
Passenger mirror cracked, held on with electrical tape
Kmart off Route Nine, shut down, whole lot, nothing but space
Foot on the clutch, foot on the gas, dumped it, the whole car hopped
He ain't grab the wheel, ain't flinch, just laughed, "that's one. From the top."
Stalled it, cranked it, stalled it, cranked it, twenty deep and I'm cussin'
He said "you treating it like a light switch, lil' bro. Listen."

[Chorus - hook established, head-nod bounce]
Clutch is a conversation, not a switch
Can't flip it, gotta feel it, let it talk back
Forty stalls in a Kmart lot, I ain't quit
Every time it catch I hear him say that

[Verse 2 - tighter delivery, tense]
Then he shipped. Civic stayed. Mirror still taped, still cracked
Black Ice tree, I keep buying 'em, so it smell like he back
Three years, Germany, six hours ahead, he call when I'm on my break
"You good?" "I'm good." "You good?" Then dead air. Then "alright, be safe."
Funny, I roll off in first now, clean, I forgot how it feel to stall
But I dump the clutch on the phone with him, every call
Got a lot of shit I wanna say, I pop it, it lurch, it die
So I ease off, let it idle. "Nah, I'm straight." And I drive.

[Chorus - scratches under hook, doubled last line]
Clutch is a conversation, not a switch
Can't flip it, gotta feel it, let it talk back
Forty stalls in a Kmart lot, I ain't quit
Every time it catch I hear him say that

[Bridge - drums out, half spoken, close mic]
Marcus. It's me. I know it's late there. Nah, nothing's wrong. Hold on.
I'm in the lot off Route Nine. Same car. Same tape. You been gone
three years and I keep stalling this part, so I'm letting it out slow...
When you back, you drive. I'ma ride shotgun. ...There. It caught.

[Final Chorus - full beat returns, doubled vocals]
Clutch is a conversation, not a switch
Can't flip it, gotta feel it, let it talk back
Forty stalls in a Kmart lot, I ain't quit
Every time it catch I hear you say that

[Outro - drums only, half spoken, hard stop]
Clutch is a conversation.
Not a switch.
[End]
```
