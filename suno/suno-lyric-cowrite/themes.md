# Theme Tensions

A song lives in a pull between two things. When grilling an artist who has no seed, offer four rows that fit the genre and anything they've said, as options a to d. Never read the list out. Once they pick, the row's Ask is the next question.

## Love
| Tension | In a life | Ask |
|---|---|---|
| shelter / lid | the thing that keeps you safe keeps you from what's past it | What protects you that you'd have to give up to get more? |
| still here / keeping score | the love is there, and so is the tally | What have you started counting? |
| the fight / the ritual | the same argument, word for word, for years | What do you two always end up fighting about? |
| wanting them / wanting to want them | the feeling left before the person did | When did you last miss them without trying? |
| protecting them / lying to them | a truth held back for their sake | What haven't you told them, and who is that for? |
| tenderness / hunger | wanting to be gentle and wanting everything | What do you want that you're careful not to ask for? |

## Family
| Tension | In a life | Ask |
|---|---|---|
| becoming them / choosing | a parent's habit showing up in your own hands | What do you do that's exactly what they did? |
| the plan / the years | a promise with a date that already passed | What did you say you'd have done by now? |
| who they remember / who you are | home still treats you like the kid who left | Who are you there that you aren't anywhere else? |
| forgiving / remembering | letting it go without pretending it didn't happen | What would forgiving them cost you? |
| holding it together / falling apart | being the steady one | Who gets to fall apart when you can't? |

## Place

| belonging / outgrowing | fitting somewhere you no longer fit | Where do you still know every name and none of the conversation? |
