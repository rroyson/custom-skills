# Suno Studio 2.0 — adding one new instrumental part to an existing song

Researched 2026-09-11. Goal: what a skill needs to write a prompt that adds ONE new instrumental part (lap steel, B3, horn stabs, counter-melody synth) to an existing v6 song already open in Studio 2.0 with stems on the timeline.
Tags: (primary) = suno.com / help.suno.com / Suno official YouTube (@suno). (secondary) = everything else. Reddit could not be fetched directly (blocked); Reddit quotes below are mirrored via secondary sites.

## 1. What Studio 2.0 is

- Launched Aug 13, 2026, Premier-only: "We have totally overhauled Suno Studio, our browser-based generative DAW". Features: "Import, record and edit MIDI directly on the timeline"; chat bar to "Generate instruments and vocals, create new and totally unique plugins and synth presets"; effects "including sidechain compression and convolution reverb"; wavetable synth; musical typing with arpeggiator/chord mode; automation. https://suno.com/release-notes/studio-2 (primary)
- Blog: "MIDI clips can also be used as a prompt for new audio generations"; Premier gets "high-quality (32-bit/48kHz) multitracks and stems" export with "no limitations"; plugin creation costs no credits at launch, "a credit structure may be introduced in the future". https://suno.com/blog/studio-2 (primary)
- Help overview: chat bar "lets you describe edits in plain language" — "adding instruments, generating sections, changing sounds, mixing, creating effects, or asking project questions". MIDI tracks hold note data; audio tracks hold sound; "drag audio onto a MIDI track" to transcribe, or "generate an audio cover of MIDI via chat". Factory effects: Compressor, Convolution, Delay, Distortion, EQ, Gate, Reverb. "Suno Studio is not compatible with VST or Audio Units plugins." https://help.suno.com/en/articles/13670529 (primary)
- Sep 2, 2026 patch: "the latest improvements make [the chat bar] aware of bpm, so now it can process tempo changes. It's also more reliable and more rational with its responses, and you can now undo prompt edits." Also fixed "MIDI and MP3 files were missing from some exports". https://suno.com/release-notes/studio-updates-sept26 (primary)
- Studio 2.0 help category (12 articles): Studio Chat, MIDI, Effects & Plugins, Wavetable Synth, Automation, Editing Clips, Take Lanes, Recording, Remove FX, Library, Shortcuts. https://help.suno.com/en/categories/2701953 (primary)
- Lineage: Studio 1.0 Sep 25, 2025 ("first-ever generative audio workstation", multitrack timeline, stem export) https://suno.com/release-notes/introducing-suno-studio (primary); 1.1 added Stem Cover + EQ https://help.suno.com/en/articles/9819905 (primary); 1.2 (Feb 16, 2026) added "Remove FX, Warp Markers, Time Signature Support and Alternates" https://suno.com/release-notes/studio-just-leveled-up (primary).
- What changed 1.x -> 2.0 (official video, Henry Phipps, PM of Studio): "We call it studio chat. And it's a chat bar that lets you talk directly to your DAW project. And that's aware of all of the audio effects that are on your tracks, it's aware of your MIDI"; "2.0 is totally backwards compatible with 1.0"; you may still "open this in the old interface". https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GZHp3WFc9Ps (primary)
- Secondary confirmation of the UI shift: "you can no longer select an instrument and say that you want to add a certain track... you can now just do that directly over here [in chat]. For example, add a guitar track." — Lanewood Studios early-access walkthrough. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xKPav7OzH7g (secondary)
- Still missing in 2.0: no pitch correction ("We don't have any sort of tuning plugin in here quite yet") https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=35AzcYYucHs (primary); gearnews calls it "a real hole" https://www.gearnews.com/suno-studio-2-0-ai-tech/ (secondary).

## 2. Exactly how you add a new generated part in Studio 2.0

- Feature name: **Studio Chat** (a.k.a. the chat bar / "Magic Bar"; beta). Open via "circular sparkle icon in the middle of the screen" or select a track/clip and press **Enter**. Two views: floating Magic Bar and a docked left-panel chat with history. Example prompts in the doc: "make the drums punchier", "add a moody synth pad under the chorus", "add a laid-back bass line that follows the drums". https://help.suno.com/en/articles/13670721 (primary)
- What chat can do (verbatim list): "Read your project", "Edit tracks and clips", "Generate music. Compose MIDI parts and generate alternate audio takes", "Build effects", "Navigate for you", "Ask before guessing. Pause mid-task to ask a clarifying question". Changes are undoable with Cmd+Z. Same URL (primary)
- Context-aware: chat is "grounded in your current context: the focused track, your selection, the project's tempo". Same URL (primary). Official video: "this can see the project state. It knows the audio that's on all of the tracks". https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GZHp3WFc9Ps (primary). Since Sep 2: "aware of bpm". (primary, release notes above)
- Official demo prompts that added a new audio part to an existing arrangement (Getting Started video): "add an ambient synth stem drone. I want it to be like lush sounding. And I just wanted to accompany what's already on the timeline"; "add another electric guitar part here like crunchy through a big amp stack" ("this is going to cook up a brand new track that's going to accompany that chorus moment"); "make this a lead electric guitar solo. Tasty". https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=35AzcYYucHs (primary). Intro video: "add a baseline here. Should be 70s inspired and electric bass". https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GZHp3WFc9Ps (primary)
- Time range: yes — select a region first. Chat placeholder reads "select a spot on the timeline and ask me to add a part" https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xKPav7OzH7g (secondary); the Suno PM selects a region before each add and says "here" in the prompt (primary, 35AzcYYucHs). Lanewood's result message: "create two funky rhodes takes on the funky keys track over the first verse". (secondary)
- Selection scope matters: "With a track selected, it edits that track. With nothing selected, it adds something new. Get that backwards and you will wonder why it keeps generating instruments you did not ask for." https://roo.beehiiv.com/p/suno-studio-2-0-midi-chat-synth (secondary); same point in https://moelueker.com/blog/suno-studio-2-tutorial-full-song-walkthrough (secondary). Help doc phrasing: "the focused track, your selection" (primary).
- Fields beside the chat text (early-access 2.0 UI): "you still have the options to add a style. For example, warm jazz guitar... Lyrics... controls of adding weirdness, stick to the style, influence more or less, and how much the available audio should influence the track to be added. And you can of course select the model that you want to use". https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xKPav7OzH7g (secondary). No primary doc names these fields for 2.0.
- Studio 1.x flow (still reachable via "open in old interface"): select a blank region -> menu "song, then lyrics, and then styles"; dropdown of "individual solo instruments, including vocals and backing vocals"; picking a non-vocal instrument (e.g. Brass) makes "the lyrics box disappear and it prompts me to put in a style"; type e.g. "solo trumpet... sparse", click Create. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WQU7r66Q26A (primary, Studio Fundamentals). Context Bar had "Advanced Options"; sliders seen: Style influence, Audio influence, Weirdness; categories incl. Woodwinds and "custom". https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OMusSwOAQhQ (secondary, Cam Dean, v1.0.0). https://help.suno.com/en/articles/7940161 (primary, archived 1.x doc).
- Audio vs MIDI: chat can "generate audio and MIDI clips onto the timeline" https://www.aimusicpreneur.com/ai-tools-news/suno-studio-2-0-midi-chat-bar-custom-plugins/ (secondary). Official MIDI video prompts: "Cover the MIDI as a grand piano sound on a new track" (MIDI->audio) and "create a powerful bassline part with MIDI that follows my track" (text->MIDI). https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N2HJUmWIbvc (primary). Say "audio" or "MIDI" explicitly.
- Target one stem (transform instead of add): select the clip and prompt e.g. "turn this into a rhythm electric guitar part" or "Keep the lyrics the same but cover this into a new lead vocal performance" — "in place it's going to replace the audio with new audio that has the same timing and melody". https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GZHp3WFc9Ps (primary). 1.x equivalent: Stem Cover ("Cover" keeps original source; "Recreate" uses current audio). https://help.suno.com/en/articles/9819905 (primary)
- Every generation yields two takes: "Every generation gives you two versions, and take lanes are where they live." Commit with Enter; thumbs-down asks "What was off?" with options incl. sound quality, timing, multiple stems. https://help.suno.com/en/articles/13670913 (primary)
- Instrumental-only toggle: none documented for 2.0. In 1.x, choosing a non-vocal instrument removed the lyrics box (primary, WQU7r66Q26A). In 2.0 "Lyrics" is just left empty for instruments (secondary, Lanewood).
- Attach reference audio: "drop it into the chat to attach it to a prompt" (library song -> chat). https://help.suno.com/en/articles/13670849 (primary)

## 3. Prompt shape for Studio generations

- Official phrasing pattern (all primary examples above): verb + ONE part + register/timbre + role vs. existing tracks + location. "add a laid-back bass line that follows the drums"; "add a moody synth pad under the chorus"; "add a baseline here. Should be 70s inspired and electric bass"; "electric guitar part here like crunchy through a big amp stack"; "ambient synth stem drone... lush... accompany what's already on the timeline". No genre tag stacks, no [section] tags, no lyrics.
- Chat expects plain language, not the Create-page Style field: "talk to Studio like you would talk to a collaborator". https://help.suno.com/en/articles/13670529 (primary)
- Name the relationship to existing tracks: "Add a piano chord melody that harmonizes with the lead vocals" — "specifying harmonization makes the AI reference existing tracks rather than inventing independently". https://moelueker.com/blog/suno-studio-2-tutorial-full-song-walkthrough (secondary)
- Say "audio" vs "MIDI" and "on a new track" explicitly (primary MIDI video: "Cover the MIDI as a grand piano sound on a new track").
- Ask for one instrument only; over-generation is a known issue: "this keyboard part really contained only the keys that I asked for because... in the previous studio version, you sometimes got a lot of extra instruments in there. Sometimes a full track even of vocals, but it actually seems to work better in Studio version 2. Although it's not perfect yet." https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xKPav7OzH7g (secondary). 1.x: "the second one added a little bit of some kind of keyboard type part in there" (Cam Dean, secondary).
- ChillPanic / Suno GPT "Studio" notes (written for v5.5-era Studio Style box, NOT the 2.0 chat): "Studio... generates one isolated element — a guitar tone, a drum pattern, a vocal performance — rather than a full song." "Style box character limit: 1,000 characters." "Has an exclude styles box. Use it; Studio often generates more instruments than requested." "Do not include genre tags in Studio prompts. Studio prompts describe sound, not songs." "Era and influence descriptors are allowed (e.g., '70s warm tube saturation,' 'vintage analog drum punch')". Example exclude list: "drums, bass, vocals, piano, synth, strings, percussion". https://github.com/borjankit/suno-workflow/blob/main/references/suno-mechanics/suno-tag-mechanics.md (secondary; "Last updated for Suno v5.5"; no mention of Studio 2.0/chat).
- Studio-Engineer repo: README badges "Studio 2.0" but contains NO Studio DAW guidance; only a claim to "generate raw MIDI data, encode it to Base64, and pass it to Suno Studio 2.0" (no evidence Studio accepts Base64 MIDI). Templates are Create-mode style/lyrics templates. https://github.com/SialkiLabs/Suno-Studio-Engineer (secondary, default branch `master`)
- Era/style words in the 1.x style field worked: "solo trumpet... sparse", "soft brushes, live drums" (primary, WQU7r66Q26A); "sparse solo trumpet" https://jackrighteous.com/en-us/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/suno-studio-add-new-instrument-stems (secondary).
- Character limit of the chat bar: not documented anywhere found. Create-page Style limit is 1,000 chars (secondary, several guides) — not confirmed to apply to chat.
- Genre tags: no primary guidance. Official demos include a genre/era cue only as a modifier ("70s inspired", "ambient") and never a tag stack. Section tags/lyrics: lyrics only apply to vocal parts (primary, 1.x video; secondary, Lanewood).

## 4. Stems

- Split modes: Auto Split "up to 12 stems — drums, bass, guitar, keyboards, woodwinds, and more", 50 credits, Pro/Premier; Split from Mix (one stem + complement), 10 credits per extraction (20 for both), Pro/Premier; Advanced Split, pick from "nearly 100 instruments", 10 credits per stem, Premier only. https://help.suno.com/en/articles/12702337 (primary); https://help.suno.com/en/articles/13925185 (primary)
- In Studio: "Right-click audio clip > Split Stems > choose split type > Extract" (primary, 13925185); Library Song Details Stems tab offers "Auto, Split-from-mix, or Custom" and "Insert all" https://help.suno.com/en/articles/13670849 (primary).
- Quality claim: stems are regenerated rather than frequency-isolated; "punchy, crisp, free of spill, and ready to drop straight into your timeline in your DAW or on Suno Studio". https://suno.com/blog/stem-separation-updates (primary, Jun 11 2026). PM: "the stems that come out of [Advanced Split] are higher fidelity" (primary, GZHp3WFc9Ps).
- Regenerate one stem: after extraction "I can audition the different versions and then choose my favorite version, or I can regenerate to extract them again." https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GmZTKZWv1BA (primary). Remove FX also yields two "dry" takes. (same)
- Caveat: "If you try to split out an instrument or vocal that is not actually present in the song, the request may still use credits, but it may not produce a usable stem." (primary, 12702337)
- Export: "All audio exports from Studio are delivered as high-quality WAV files"; Full Song / Selected Time Range / Multitrack (zip of per-track WAVs); right-click clip -> Download WAV or MIDI; Get MIDI costs 10 credits per stem. https://help.suno.com/en/articles/8128193 (primary); 32-bit/48kHz (primary, blog/studio-2). Studio downloads exempt from the Sep 3, 2026 download caps: "users will be able to download their work from Studio without limitation". https://help.suno.com/en/articles/13614785 (primary)
- Failure modes seen/reported:
  - Timing: generated part "a little bit ahead of the beat... something that we're trying to work out on our side" — fix by holding Cmd and nudging (primary, WQU7r66Q26A, 1.x); 2.0 synth take: "the timing got a little messed up in the middle" (primary, 35AzcYYucHs).
  - Extra instruments/vocals in an instrument take (secondary, Lanewood; Cam Dean) — see section 3.
  - Instrument-category confusion (1.x): "sometimes it thinks that trumpets are woodwinds" (primary, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qR4BefPvSiI).
  - Time signature "is not yet sent to generative models when creating new clips" (1.2 note). https://help.suno.com/en/articles/10625089 (primary)
  - 2.0 walkthrough: "Backing vocal harmonies drifting out of sync (regenerating the entire vocal worked better)"; "AI-generated MIDI bass patterns being too simplistic". https://moelueker.com/blog/suno-studio-2-tutorial-full-song-walkthrough (secondary)
  - Pre-2.0 Reddit reports (mirrored): "v5 full stem split: stem bleed is worse than before" (r/SunoAI, Sep 25 2025); stem replacement "worked 2/100 times... literally creates an entirely... different song" (r/SunoAI, Apr 24 2026); drum stems "with reverb that wasn't in the original". https://undetectr.com/blog/suno-studio-review (secondary, May 19 2026, describes Studio 1.x/v5).
  - Tempo drift when BPM set to "Follow Track" instead of manual BPM. https://www.jgbeatslab.com/ai-music-lab-blog/suno-studio-fix-tempo-drift-daw-sync (secondary)
  - Key mismatch: no source documents key-awareness or key failures either way.

## 5. Credits, tiers, limits

- Studio is Premier-only: $24/mo billed annually, "10,000 credits per month", "Suno Studio - now with MIDI, effects, automation, and design-your-own-plugins", "3 stem separation types". Pro ($8/mo annual, 2,500 credits) has no Studio. https://suno.com/pricing (primary). Monthly Premier is $30. https://www.musicradar.com/music-tech/sunos-studio-2-0-adds-an-ai-chatbot-that-can-control-your-project-transform-sounds-and-generate-custom-plugins (secondary)
- Known per-action costs (primary): Auto Split 50; Split from Mix / Advanced Split 10 per stem (12702337); Get MIDI 10 per stem (8128193); plugin creation 0 at launch (13670785); generic generation "two songs with a total cost of 10 credits" https://help.suno.com/en/articles/13924481 (primary, v6 FAQ — Create page, not stated for Studio chat).
- Studio chat audio generation cost: no primary figure. Secondary: "Every regenerate, every Alternates request, every stem replacement, every 'add this to that section' prompt costs credits... fixing a single song through iteration can burn 200-500 credits" (undetectr, pre-2.0); "regenerations and stem replacements are where the 10,000 credits actually disappear" https://roo.beehiiv.com/p/suno-midi-export-cost (secondary).
- Length of a generated part: no Studio-specific limit documented. v6 Create generations: "up to 8 minutes per generation". https://help.suno.com/en/articles/13924801 (primary)
- Model: v6 launched Sep 9, 2026 (v6, v6-wild Pro/Premier; v6-mini all). https://suno.com/blog/introducing-v6 (primary). The v6 help pages do not mention Studio. A model picker exists in the Studio chat (secondary, Lanewood).
